## English caption

MVMCMD is a command-driven Android utility app built around transparent action states. Camera, QR, wallpaper and English tools sit beside a command engine that distinguishes **STARTED** from **VERIFIED** instead of faking completion.

This is an internal candidate preview, not a claim of universal device support or measured battery/FPS improvements. Some capabilities depend on Android permissions and OEM behavior.
