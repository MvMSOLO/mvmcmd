## Recording rules

- Capture screen content directly from the running app; do not create fake completion states.
- Use a test device/account and synthetic content. Hide phone numbers, contact names, notification text, account IDs, and unrelated personal UI.
- Do not claim universal OEM support, guaranteed battery savings, increased FPS, or perfect automation.
- If a permission prompt or restricted setting blocks the flow, record the truthful guidance or replace the shot with another verified scenario.
- Keep a separate unedited source recording for audit and capture the app commit / build version in production notes.
